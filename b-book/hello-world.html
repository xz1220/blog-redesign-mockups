What this blog is for

Notes on building AI agents, multi-agent systems, AI products, and knowledge work.

邢政·2026-06-01·1 min·更新于 2026-08-31

This site is where I keep notes on the AI agents and products I build: what they do, how they work, and the decisions behind them.

I’m most interested in practical agent runtimes, multi-agent systems, AI products, and knowledge work—especially the infrastructure that lets solo builders put agent systems into production.

Right now, I’m building Open Dynamic Workflows, a runtime for portable AI-agent workflows. That is where many of the first notes will begin.
